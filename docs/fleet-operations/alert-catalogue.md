# Alert catalogue

:::info[Generated page]
Generated by `scripts/gen-alert-catalogue.mjs` from `alerting/warehouse-alert-rules.json`, a copy of warehouse-infra `terraform/alerting/warehouse-alert-rules.json` at `c6a4d76`. Do not edit this page: change the rule generator in warehouse-infra (`scripts/gen-context-dashboards.py`), then run `npm run gen-alert-catalogue -- --sync` here.
:::

The fleet provisions **185 alert rules** in **60 rule groups** (two per
bounded context: the coarse rules and the SLO burn-rate rules). warehouse-infra loads them into the
`prometheus-community/prometheus` chart as `serverFiles."alerting_rules.yml"`
(`terraform/10-platform/observability.tf`). This cluster runs the plain chart without the
Prometheus Operator, so there are no `PrometheusRule` objects: the rules are a
rule file. `make alert-rules-check` in warehouse-infra runs `promtool check rules`
and the unit tests in `terraform/alerting/tests/` (`warehouse-alert-rules.test.yml`,
`warehouse-slo-burn-rules.test.yml`).

:::note[Nothing pages anyone]
Alertmanager is on in the kind cluster, but its only receiver is `null`: it
groups, inhibits and shows alerts (`/api/v2/alerts`) and **delivers nothing**,
because a local cluster has no pager. A firing alert shows in Alertmanager, on
Prometheus' `/alerts` page, in the `ALERTS` series and in Grafana. Every rule
carries a `severity` (`warning`, or `critical` for the fast SLO burn alerts), a
`context` label, a `runbook_url` and a
`dashboard_url` annotation. See [Fleet SLOs](./slos.md) for the objectives these
alerts protect and the [cross-context incident runbook](./incident-runbook.md)
for what to do when one fires.
:::

## Rule kinds

| Alert | For | Fires when | Contexts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `WarehouseKong5xxRatioHigh` | 5m | more than 5% of the requests on one of the context's Kong routes (API, MCP or reports) answered 5xx over the last 5 minutes | 30 |
| `WarehouseOutboxLagHigh` | 5m | the oldest unpublished `outbox_events` row is older than 60 s (the context's outbox-lag gauge) | 5 |
| `WarehousePodRestarting` | 0m | a container of one of the context's pods restarted 2 or more times in 15 minutes (kube-state-metrics) | 30 |
| `WarehouseApiAvailabilityBurnFast` | 2m | SLO 1 burn rate is 14.4 or more over 1 h and 5 m (5xx share of 14.4% or more): the day's budget is gone in about 100 minutes; `critical` | 30 |
| `WarehouseApiAvailabilityBurnSlow` | 15m | SLO 1 burn rate is 3 or more over 6 h and 30 m (5xx share of 3% or more): the day's budget is gone in about 8 hours; `warning` | 30 |
| `WarehouseApiLatencyBurnFast` | 2m | SLO 2 burn rate is 14.4 or more over 1 h and 5 m (more than 72% of requests slower than 400 ms upstream); `critical` | 30 |
| `WarehouseApiLatencyBurnSlow` | 15m | SLO 2 burn rate is 3 or more over 6 h and 30 m (more than 15% of requests slower than 400 ms upstream); `warning` | 30 |

Why some signals have no rule (from the generator's own notes):

- **Outbox lag** has a rule only where the context exports an outbox-lag gauge.
  No context exports an outbox dead-row (attempt-cap) metric: a row parked by
  `OUTBOX_MAX_ATTEMPTS` shows up as an ERROR log line and in SQL only.
- **Consumer lag** has no rule: no Kafka exporter runs in the cluster, so no
  consumer-group lag metric exists. Check lag with `kafka-consumer-groups.sh`
  (see the [incident runbook](./incident-runbook.md)).
- **5xx** comes from Kong only: `otelchi` records no status code on
  `http.server.request.duration`, so a request that never passes Kong (a
  pod-to-pod call) is not counted.

### order-management

Rule groups `warehouse-order-management` and `warehouse-order-management-slo`.

**`WarehouseKong5xxRatioHigh`** (for `5m`, severity `warning`): Order Management: more than 5% of requests on a Kong route answer 5xx.

```text
sum by (service) (rate(kong_http_requests_total{service=~"httproute[.]warehouse-systems[.]order-management(-mcp|-reports)?[.][0-9]+", code=~"5.."}[5m])) / sum by (service) (rate(kong_http_requests_total{service=~"httproute[.]warehouse-systems[.]order-management(-mcp|-reports)?[.][0-9]+"}[5m])) > 0.05
```

Description annotation:

```text
Kong route {{ $labels.service }} answered 5xx for {{ $value | humanizePercentage }} of its requests over the last 5 minutes (API route = <context>.N, MCP = <context>-mcp.N, reports = <context>-reports.N).
```

**`WarehouseOutboxLagHigh`** (for `5m`, severity `warning`): Order Management: the transactional outbox is not draining.

```text
max by (service_name) (order_outbox_lag_seconds{service_name=~"order-management.*"}) > 60
```

Description annotation:

```text
The oldest unpublished outbox_events row of {{ $labels.service_name }} is {{ $value | humanizeDuration }} old. Kafka is unreachable or a head row keeps failing until OUTBOX_MAX_ATTEMPTS parks it; downstream contexts stop seeing this context's events. The runbook has the re-arm SQL.
```

**`WarehousePodRestarting`** (for `0m`, severity `warning`): Order Management: a container is restarting.

```text
sum by (pod, container) (increase(kube_pod_container_status_restarts_total{namespace="warehouse-systems", pod=~"order-management-.+"}[15m])) >= 2
```

Description annotation:

```text
{{ $labels.pod }}/{{ $labels.container }} restarted {{ $value | humanize }} times in 15 minutes (boot-retry exhaustion, a failed migration, a stopped consumer that exits on purpose, or an OOM kill). Read the previous container's last log lines first.
```

