# Model Gap Register

The ten context packs were written strictly from
[`model/model.yaml`](https://github.com/IQVO/retail-network-docs/blob/main/model/model.yaml).
Where the model was silent or contradicted itself, the pack did not invent an
answer: it marked the choice "proposed" or "this pack's reading" on the page and
reported a gap. 2026-10-22 produced about 140 reports. This page holds them
deduplicated into 36 items.

**The model carries the as-built contracts, not every resolution below.** The
events, fields, consumers and routes of the ten services are in `model.yaml`
(checked against each service's specification). The Status column says whether a
service built the proposed resolution; the resolution text of a Closed row is what was actually decided. A gap is
Closed only when the aggregates, invariants and policies it names are also edited
in `model.yaml` and `scripts/check_model.py` passes.

Each gap row ends with a **Status**, read from the ADRs and specs of the ten
services (`docs/adr/0002-aggregates-and-invariants.md` and later) after they were
built from this register. Of the 36 gaps, **7 are Closed** (the decision is built
and written into `model.yaml`, and `scripts/check_model.py` enforces what it can)
and **29 are Built** as proposed, with their aggregates, invariants and policies
not yet edited in the model. None is Open.

Rows G41 to G55 were added with [ADR 0009](/network/adr/0009-seller-inbound-and-case-packed-shipments)
(seller inbound and case-packed shipments). They describe what that design
introduces and does not solve. No service has been built from them, so all 15
are **Open**; the counts above cover G1 to G40 only.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Closed** | Built, and the model says what was built. The note names where in `model.yaml` and the repo ADR assumption. G3, G9, G11, G12 and G40 were built differently from the proposal; their resolution cell now states the decision. G36 and G37 are enforced by `check_model.py` (rules 5 and 6). |
| **Built** | A service implemented the proposed resolution as written. The note names the repo, its ADR and the assumption (A followed by a number). |

Severity:

| Severity | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **S1** | A cross-context flow cannot work as modelled: a policy has no data, or a fact never reaches the context that needs it. |
| **S2** | A race or failure path has no compensation: the happy path works, the unhappy one strands an aggregate. |
| **S3** | Incomplete inside one context: a missing transition, formula, vocabulary or route owner. |
| **S4** | Convention gaps shared by every context. |

## S2: races and failure paths with no compensation

| # | Gap | Contexts | Proposed resolution | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G12 | Cancel race: `order-sourcing` cancels a `RELEASED` assignment while the vendor is accepting its PO. `vendor-connectivity` refuses to cancel (`ErrCancelAfterAcknowledgement`), but no one is told, so the vendor may ship a cancelled group. | customer-ordering, order-sourcing, vendor-connectivity | `CustomerOrderCancelled` is a request. `order-sourcing` decides: it publishes `FulfillmentCancelled` for a `QUEUED`, `RELEASED` or `UNSOURCEABLE` assignment, and `FulfillmentCancelRefused` (reason `CANCEL_AFTER_LOCK`) for a `LOCKED`, `SHIPPED` or `DELIVERED` one. The lock is the vendor's acceptance of the purchase order. After a refusal the vendor ships and the shopper's refusal becomes a return. `customer-ordering` consumes both events and shows the outcome; its own `ErrCancelAfterLock` is advisory. `vendor-connectivity` cancels the purchase order on `FulfillmentCancelled`; if the vendor has already acknowledged it, it logs `ErrCancelAfterAcknowledgement` and publishes nothing. One race stays open: an acceptance that arrives after `order-sourcing` cancelled a `RELEASED` assignment is only logged, so the vendor may still ship a cancelled group. | Closed - model: contexts[order-sourcing].aggregates[FulfillmentAssignment] (transitions, invariants `ErrCancelAfterLock`, commands `RefuseCancel`, events, notes), .policies[CancelDecision, AcceptanceAfterCancelOnlyLogged]; contexts[customer-ordering].aggregates[CustomerOrder] (invariants, commands), .policies[CancelIsARequest]; contexts[vendor-connectivity].policies[CancelOnSourcingCancel]; build: order-sourcing ADR 0002 A7; customer-ordering ADR 0002 A3; vendor-connectivity ADR 0002 A15 (the build wins: the refusal follows the lock, and the open race, an acceptance that arrives after a cancel, is recorded in the model as a known residual and is only logged) |

3. Update the affected context packs and OpenAPI files. Remove the "proposed" or "this pack's reading" marker where the model now decides.
